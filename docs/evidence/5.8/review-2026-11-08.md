# 5.8 review, 8 Nov 2026

An adversarial review of a38ab85 (5.8, L0 asset handoff by local path) ran
four lenses: correctness, Windows paths and security, concurrency and the
engine, and test validity. Three refuters checked each finding, and a finding
survived when at least two could not refute it. 17 findings were raised and
13 survived. Twelve are fixed below. The thirteenth is older than 5.8 and
became row 5.11 by user decision.

## Became row 5.11 (user decision, 8 Nov)

After a scene with a font is published, by path or chunked, a killed worker
cannot restart: "cannot restore recovery journal: … prepared scene … cannot be
rebuilt: asset missing: 3127f0b8…", exit 1. The fault dates from 5.9, not 5.8:
since 5.9 the engine keeps verified bytes only in memory, the journal stores
only their hashes, and restore rebuilds every scene with an empty asset map.
The agent reproduced this on both routes with a throwaway integration test
against the real worker; the test was not committed.

- Decision (Jev and Laya both): a new row, fixed before 5.10. Rows 3.12 and
  5.9 keep their done status, with a note.
- Design (user, with Jev; Laya was near-random): persist verified bytes in an
  engine-owned content store and re-hash them on restore. A scene whose bytes
  are missing or fail their hash is dropped with a named reason, and the
  engine still starts output-inhibited.
- Package total limit: 4 GiB (Jev and Laya both).

Questions and answers: `review-jev-laya.json`.

## Executed

- `cargo test -p gx-asset-plane`: handoff 10 of 10 and package 11 of 11 pass.
- Mutation, the a38ab85 path check restored: three new handoff tests fail
  (spelling, links, resolved path in the detail). Restored byte-identical.
- Mutation, share pre-check removed: the seam test fails, having resolved
  `\\gx-no-such-host.invalid\share\…`, `\\127.0.0.1\…`, `\\.\NUL` and
  `\\?\GLOBALROOT\…`. Restored byte-identical.
- Mutation, the a38ab85 reader restored: the ZIP64 test aborts the test
  process ("memory allocation of 17592186044416 bytes failed",
  0xc0000409), and the total-limit and over-length tests fail. Restored
  byte-identical.
- Mutation, a fresh transfer counter per connection: the engine-side byte
  assertion fails (0 against 40). Restored byte-identical.
- Mutation, the co-located worker started with a misspelled flag: conformance
  exits 1 with "FAIL peer starts with the suite's arguments … exited with exit
  code: 2 … unknown argument". Before the fix this was a SKIP. Restored
  byte-identical.
- Conformance: 544 passed, 0 failed, 9 skipped. That is 543 plus the
  real-worker link check.
- Timing was tried as the test for the share pre-check and dropped: on this
  machine the SMB call to a share that does not exist failed in 10 to 40 ms
  under both the old code and the new, so timing could not tell them apart.
