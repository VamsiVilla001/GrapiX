3. The external review, checked against our evidence

  • Moments replace the timeline: disputed. B3 already says keyframes exist one level down. The title "motion without a timeline" invites the misreading. Real gaps: no rule for re-applying a preset over edited curves, and times shown in ms while the engine is frame-exact.
  • Engine phases: the engine has five (In, Hold, Out, Continue, Update). Our four Moments and the review's Enter, Hold, Update, Exit both leave out Continue.
  • Workspaces: disputed. The owner decision is toggles plus named saved layouts. The gap is that the Layout dropdown is not drawn. The shipped Editor has 8 menus, not 6 or 10.
  • Ecosystem diagram: adds DataHub as an application and draws no engine behind Playout. It contradicts invariants 1, 2, 5 and 6.
  • Right, and we should change: B11 lists AI clients by model name, but the invariants grant by credential scope. The TAKE guarantee needs a per-field live-update exception.
  • Confirmed: Scene ID (same model, clearer wording), "Editor controls schema, Playout controls values", and "JavaScript first", which is our own earlier decision.
  • Playout list: planned: import/export, Stream Deck, acknowledgement and dedup. Out of scope for release 1: multiple outputs, mirrored failover. Missing: groups, bulk IDs, emergency graphics.
  • Missed entirely: accessibility, performance budgets, error and stale states, keyboard operation, TAKE and CLEAR safety. Its seven ratings are its own opinions, not measurements.
